Beds on this tablet

Records hold clinical data and a bed number only, never a name, CHI or date of birth, and auto-delete after 21 days. Two tablets do not share records: a patient admitted on the other tablet shows no baseline here, so use the admission sheet in the notes. Clear a bed record once the discharge sheet is filed.
